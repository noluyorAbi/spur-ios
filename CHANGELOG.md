# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- Map types (Standard, Satellit, Hybrid) and a frequency heatmap of the driven roads.
- Planen: search places and streets, see how much of a street you have driven, plan
  discovery loops and routes to a goal over roads you have not driven, with live progress.
- Automatic pause from the location alone, manual pause, and pause time in the statistics.
- Gap fill: the likely route across a GPS gap, accepted or rejected per gap.
- Divided roads driven both ways count as both directions, frontage roads included.

## 1.3.0

- Live details over the map while recording: acceleration circle, values, recent events.

## 1.2.0

- Motion sensor recording: braking, acceleration, cornering, bumps, road surface; Live Activity.

## 1.1.0

- OpenStreetMap road data: signals, stop signs, skewed junctions, speed limits, road coverage
  by direction.

## 1.0.0

- Background GPS recording, trip history, statistics, exports (GPX, CSV, GeoJSON, JSON, ZIP).
